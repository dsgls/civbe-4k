# Event/quest/etc dialogs

On "resource pod searched", "Quest received", "Marvel discovered" and other such dialogs, the frame too small vertically. The objective (checkbox and "Find 2 Resource Pods (0 found)") is below the, with no background. Sometimes the event text itself is also below the frame, backgroundless. The "OK" bubtton is inside the too-small frame, and when the text is inside the dialog but too long, the "OK" button will cover part of the text.
